Early 1990s

Manchester United enjoyed immediate success in the Premier League era, winning their first league title in the inaugural season of 1992-1993 under the management of Sir Alex Ferguson.

Mid 1990s

The club went on to secure a "hat-trick" of Premier League titles in the 1993-1994, 1994-1995, and 1995-1996 seasons, including memorable League and FA Cup "Doubles" in 1994 and 1996.

Late 1990s

During this era, Manchester United also achieved the famous "Treble" in the 1998-1999 season, winning the Premier League, FA Cup, and UEFA Champions League in the same campaign.

Early 2000s

United continued their domestic dominance at the turn of the millennium, winning Premier League titles in 1999-2000, 2000-2001, and 2002-2003, powered by prolific goalscorer Ruud van Nistelrooy and the iconic Class of '92.

Mid 2000s

A period of rebuilding under Sir Alex Ferguson brought the arrivals of Wayne Rooney, Cristiano Ronaldo, Nemanja Vidić, and Patrice Evra, culminating in the 2003-2004 FA Cup, the 2005-2006 League Cup, and the return of the Premier League trophy in 2006-2007.

Late 2000s

Led by Ballon d'Or winner Cristiano Ronaldo and Wayne Rooney, Manchester United won three consecutive Premier League titles from 2006 to 2009, conquered Europe in the 2007-2008 UEFA Champions League final in Moscow, and lifted the FIFA Club World Cup in 2008.

Early 2010s

Manchester United surpassed Liverpool's record with their 19th league title in 2010-2011 and captured an historic 20th top-flight crown in 2012-2013—inspired by Robin van Persie—marking a triumphant farewell for legendary manager Sir Alex Ferguson after 26 years in charge.

Mid 2010s

Following Sir Alex Ferguson's retirement, the club navigated a period of transition under David Moyes and Louis van Gaal, winning the 2015-2016 FA Cup at Wembley before appointing José Mourinho to lead a new push for major silverware.

Late 2010s

In 2016-2017, Manchester United lifted the FA Community Shield, EFL Cup, and their first-ever UEFA Europa League trophy in Stockholm, completing the full set of major European club honors before club legend Ole Gunnar Solskjær returned as manager in late 2018.

Early 2020s

Sparked by the arrival of Bruno Fernandes and the return of Cristiano Ronaldo, United achieved back-to-back top-three Premier League finishes and reached the 2021 UEFA Europa League final before Erik ten Hag took charge and ended a six-year trophy drought with the 2022-2023 Carabao Cup.

Today

After triumphing over rivals Manchester City to win the 2023-2024 FA Cup—the club's 13th FA Cup title—Manchester United continues to build for the future, blending world-class talent with celebrated academy graduates in pursuit of returning to the pinnacle of English and European football.
